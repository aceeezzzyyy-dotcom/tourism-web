COAST & SEA

Coastal Escape

2 days / 1 night · Couples, friends, beach lovers

Enjoy a relaxed coastal getaway with time for beach visits and local dining around Puerto Galera.

Sample itinerary

  • Day 1: Arrive, check in, explore the beach, enjoy a local dinner.
  • Day 2: Morning by the sea, optional water activity, depart.

Sample inclusions: itinerary guide and destination suggestions.

Not included: transport, lodging, meals, entrance fees, and optional activities.

Estimated budget: ₱2,500–₱5,000 per person

NATURE & ADVENTURE

Highland Nature Break

2 days / 1 night · Active travelers, nature lovers

A nature-focused itinerary with scenic stops and responsible outdoor exploration.

Sample itinerary

  • Day 1: Travel inland, enjoy scenic stops, overnight at a chosen accommodation.
  • Day 2: Take a guided nature activity suited to current conditions, then return.

Sample inclusions: planning checklist and suggested activities.

Not included: guide fees, permits, transport, lodging, meals, and equipment.

Estimated budget: ₱3,000–₱6,000 per person

SLOW TRAVEL

Mindful Mindoro

3 days / 2 nights · Families, slow travelers

Mix peaceful nature stops, local food, and flexible time to discover the province at an easy pace.

Sample itinerary

  • Day 1: Arrive and settle in; explore nearby community attractions.
  • Day 2: Visit a nature destination such as the Naujan Lake area, subject to access guidance.
  • Day 3: Enjoy a local breakfast, pick up souvenirs, and depart.

Sample inclusions: suggested itinerary and travel reminders.

Not included: transport, accommodation, meals, entrance fees, and purchases.

Estimated budget: ₱4,000–₱8,000 per person
